Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 2026

Your Privacy Matters

Text Capsule is built with your privacy in mind. We collect only the minimum information needed to deliver your future messages.

What We Collect

  • Your email address (for account login)
  • Your phone number (to deliver messages via SMS)
  • The messages you write (stored securely until delivery)
  • Your delivery preferences

How We Use Your Data

Your data is used solely to provide the Text Capsule service. We use your phone number to deliver your messages at the scheduled time via Twilio (our messaging provider). We do not sell, share, or use your data for advertising.

Message Storage

Your messages are stored securely in our database until they are delivered. After delivery, messages remain in your account history so you can look back on them. You can delete any message at any time.

Third-Party Services

We use Twilio to send SMS messages. When a message is delivered, your phone number and message content are shared with Twilio for the purpose of delivery only. We also use RevenueCat to manage subscriptions. Please refer to their respective privacy policies for more information.

Subscriptions

Text Capsule offers auto-renewable subscriptions managed through Apple's App Store. Payment is processed by Apple. We use RevenueCat to track subscription status but do not store your payment details.

Data Security

Your password is securely hashed and never stored in plain text. All communication between the app and our servers is encrypted. We take reasonable measures to protect your personal information.

Your Rights

  • View all your stored messages at any time
  • Delete any message before or after delivery
  • Update your phone number and delivery preferences
  • Delete your account and all associated data from within the app

Contact Us

If you have any questions about this privacy policy or how we handle your data, please contact us at simonaletta@hotmail.co.uk.
